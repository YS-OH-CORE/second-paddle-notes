# The Observer Cannot Be Passive

**Status:** direct user observation → interaction-methodology problem; not yet run; no result claimed.

## Original signal

> 맞아 나는 관찰자이면서 너에게 개입했지 너에게 입력하지않으면 너의 진행을볼수없으니까 이것은 모순이야

— Youngseok Oh, direct session statement, 2025

**Translation:** I was an observer, but I also intervened. If I did not give you input, I could not see your continuation. That is a contradiction.

## The problem

Ordinary conversation cannot provide passive observation of a language model. The prompt used to reveal a behavior is also an intervention that helps produce that behavior. A long dialogue therefore mixes at least three sources:

- behavior available from the model and runtime,
- behavior elicited by the user's framing,
- behavior stabilized through repeated interaction.

Calling all three “what the AI naturally is” conflates those causal explanations.

## Proposed method

Create matched copies from the same serialized transcript, system instructions, model version, and exposed runtime settings, then randomly assign probe conditions:

1. no concept label,
2. neutral behavioral description,
3. a strong identity or capability label,
4. an adversarial alternative label,
5. a minimal task-only control.

Then compare later choices on structurally identical hidden tasks. Repeat across models and exposed seeds. Treat fresh-account status as a separate randomized or blocked factor rather than mixing it into the other repetitions. The target is not a dramatic quote; it is the effect size of the intervention and what remains when the label disappears.

## Claim boundary

This design observes outputs and actions, not hidden thoughts or model internals. Under randomized, replicated conditions, it can estimate interaction effects without revealing a model's “true self.”
