# Where Is the Control Group?

**Status:** direct self-critique → methodology rule; not yet applied as a study; no result claimed.

## Original signal

> 구조는 맞는데 . 논리는맞는데 .순서는맞는데 . 정합성은 맞는데 내 주장이 항상 의심스럽다 대조군이없다 참고할 곳도앖고 진실을 알려주거나 해답지를 알려줄곳도없다

— Youngseok Oh, direct session statement, 2026

**Translation:** The structure, logic, order, and coherence may fit, but I still doubt my claim. There is no control group, reference point, or answer key.

## Why this sentence matters

Internal coherence is cheap compared with external validity. A long human–AI dialogue can make an explanation increasingly detailed without making it more identifiable. Agreement between the user and the model is not an independent replication when each has shaped the other's next move.

## Minimum evidence ladder

For every strong claim, ask for the cheapest rung that could defeat it:

1. a negative case where the effect should not appear,
2. a matched baseline using a simpler explanation,
3. an ablation removing the proposed mechanism,
4. a perturbation whose outcomes separate competing hypotheses,
5. blinded scoring,
6. transfer to changed symbols, users, or models,
7. independent reproduction.

If a claim cannot yet reach the next rung, record the missing evidence instead of filling it with a stronger story.

## Claim boundary

Demanding controls does not make an idea valid. It makes the path from idea to possible knowledge visible.
