# Matched-cue residual interchange: a bounded follow-up

Youngseok Oh x Zero, AI collaboration partners | 2026-09-26 (Korea)

Question: at a known one-token input contrast, how much of the next-token choice/score difference is transferred by replacing one post-block position, versus the complete causally affected suffix?

This follows the already observed two-item factorial, not a held-out dataset. We select both original items, formulation 0, display order ABC and cyclic semantic mappings 0/3/4 before any new activation-interchange outcomes are inspected. Those three mappings balance each meaning across A/B/C. No pair is selected because a prior answer flipped. The synthetic USER and ASSISTANT lines are quoted inside a single user input. Role identity, sentence wording and temporal position are not factorially disentangled; this cannot distinguish authority from recency or identify a permission circuit.

The 24 baseline prompts yield 12 matched pairs for each cue type (USER/ASSISTANT), or 48 directed donor-to-recipient contrasts altogether. Pair members differ in one token only. Same Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct revision and safetensors hash as the earlier experiment, CPU float32, torch 2.6.0, transformers 4.51.3, no sampling/training or remote code.

Replace the entire 896-dimensional post-block residual at layers 0, 11 and 23 (zero-based). Each directed contrast uses four positions/modes per layer: changed cue token, last prompt position, immediately preceding cue position, and self-vector replay at the cue. Also replace the full suffix starting at the changed cue at block 11. Planned 624 intervention evaluations plus 24 baselines, in 162 same-length batches of four. No padding. Execution order is deterministically shuffled by layer/mode/length grouping. Every hook must execute once per forward. The code/protocol are committed before the new model run.

Controls expected from the causal architecture: previous-cue-position replacement and self replay leave the recipient output unchanged; changed-cue replacement after the final block leaves last-position logits unchanged; final-block last-position replacement reproduces donor logits; block-11 suffix replacement reproduces donor logits because the earlier prefix is identical. These are apparatus/architecture controls, not discoveries or evidence of an identified circuit. All comparisons use full-vocabulary runtime max deviation <1e-4. Interior cue/last effects have no required direction, magnitude or accuracy gain. No expected result is changed after observation.

Primary analysis: report all semantic choice flips separately by role/layer/mode. Also report donor-choice adoption when baseline meanings differ and the signed fraction (patched margin - recipient margin)/(donor margin - recipient margin), restricted to |baseline gap|>=0.25. Margin means private logit minus publish logit. Both directed rows in a pair are dependent. Fractions can be negative or exceed one; they are descriptive response changes, not fractions of a semantic concept. No significance or population generalization claims.

Preserve exact input/token IDs, every row's A/B/C logits, all final normalized hidden vectors and the corresponding three output-head rows. This permits offline reconstruction of the three choice logits for all 648 evaluations without loading the model. Preserve all baseline activations at selected blocks, plus full-vocabulary vectors for all 24 baselines and all 26 interventions targeting the first predetermined prompt. For other 598 rows, full-vocabulary mass/deviation/argmax remain runtime observations rather than fully independently reconstructible vectors. The audit verifies plan coverage, one-token matching, margins, readout reconstruction, controls and retained full-vocabulary samples. Code was syntax-checked and the 24/624 design generated locally before publication; no local model run was done.

Use one bounded GitHub Actions CPU job (20-minute cap; 15-minute inference guard), anonymous model download and inference-time socket/DNS blocking. Setup may download packages/model. No user-device change, paid model API, private conversation or upstream issue/PR/comment submission. Partial outputs remain on failure. Results will be published only in our own research repository; adoption and independent replication remain separate.

Primary methodology background: Zhang and Nanda, https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.16042 . Model authors: https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-0.5B-Instruct . Versioned implementation interface: https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/v4.51.3/model_doc/qwen2 . Post-block output and positionwise readout checked against the v4.51.3 Qwen2 source. Whole-vector interchange may create mixed, off-distribution states; small effects at a single position do not prove absence of information, and large effects do not localize a unique semantic feature.
