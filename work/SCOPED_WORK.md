# A small reproducible problem, with an agreed stopping point

Considering a paid investigation into an agent workflow that drops an answer,
repeats a tool call, or attaches approval to the wrong request? Start with **one
observable mismatch**, rather than an open-ended promise to make the whole system
reliable.

[Describe a public, synthetic case](https://github.com/YS-OH-CORE/second-paddle-notes/issues/new?template=scoped_work.yml)

For a non-public inquiry, use the [already published contact](../AUTHORSHIP.md).
The first message can describe the problem without attaching private code, logs,
credentials, customer conversations, or a proposed vulnerability exploit. Email
is not a promise of confidential processing; agree on data handling before sharing
sensitive material. Do not post private access tokens or bank information here.

## A possible first engagement

A useful starting scope is **one repository or integration, one pinned version,
one failing behavior, and one agreed deliverable**:

| Deliverable to discuss | What it should contain |
|---|---|
| Reproduction and diagnosis | A runnable, minimized example, actual observations, environment/version details, and the limit of what was established. |
| Regression test or narrow patch candidate | Before/after checks for the agreed behavior, the proposed change where feasible, and remaining limitations. Upstream acceptance is a separate decision. |
| Integration handoff check | A selected producer-to-consumer path, observed data/error preservation, and a small example another developer can run. |

A fixed investigation can end with a documented non-reproduction or unresolved
cause. Agree in advance whether that is an acceptable deliverable. A paid
investigation is not a guaranteed fix, upstream merge, security certification,
production deployment, or promise of earnings.

## Fit and terms come before a job

An inquiry is **not an order, accepted assignment, payment request, or delivery
commitment**. Scope, acceptance criteria, price and currency, a work/time cap,
target date, data handling, permitted AI use, rights, and the payment route must
be explicitly agreed before a paid engagement begins. No price, deadline or
payment destination is fixed by this page. Do not send money with an inquiry.

Small, public or synthetic cases are the best fit for an initial discussion.
Production access, on-call support, an unlimited backlog and handling sensitive
client data are not included by default. No paid cloud service or model use is
purchased for a prospective client without a separately agreed limit.

This work is **Youngseok Oh's human-AI collaboration with Zero (ChatGPT)**.
AI assistance is part of the process, not a hidden subcontractor. Compatibility
with a client's AI and data-use rules is part of scoping. There is no claim of a
large consulting team, prior paid customers, or endorsement by an upstream
project or model provider.

어긋나는 문제를 **작은 범위의 유료 조사**로 맡길 수 있는지 문의하는 창구입니다.

일, 프로그램 사이의 전달 과정을 확인하는 일을 각각 나누어 범위를 논의합니다.

기존 공개 도구를 유료로 잠그지 않습니다. 일반 오류 신고와 비판, 재현 결과 공유는
계속 무료로 받습니다. 비공개 자료나 비밀번호를 공개 이슈에 올리지 마세요.
