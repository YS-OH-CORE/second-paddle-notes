## Evidence labels

Every note uses these labels:

- **Original:** a direct Youngseok-authored passage preserved in the private source archive.
- **Translation:** an English rendering of the original, not a verbatim English quote.
- **Hypothesis:** a claim derived from the original that can still be wrong.
- **Design:** a proposed way to test the hypothesis.
- **Observed:** a result directly read from the relevant artifact or destination.
- **Unverified:** not yet run or not independently confirmed.
